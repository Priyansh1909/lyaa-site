Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version. Lyaa has no account and no servers that store your health data. What you log stays on your iPhone. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We never sell or share your data.

This policy explains what the Lyaa app (“Lyaa”, “we”) does with information. Lyaa is made by the Lyaa developer. If anything here is unclear, write to Blankmess99@gmail.com.

What you log stays on your device

Your periods, symptoms, moods, temperatures, tests, notes, workouts and settings are stored on your iPhone. They are protected by your device's encryption and passcode. We have no copy and cannot see them.

Optional: iCloud sync (Premium)

If you turn on iCloud sync, Lyaa keeps your data in your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so it stays the same on your devices and survives a new phone. This data is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access your private iCloud database. You can turn sync off at any time in Lyaa (You › Sync), and you can manage iCloud data in your iPhone's Settings.

Optional: Apple Health

If you allow it, Lyaa reads past period dates from Apple Health and saves the period starts you log in Lyaa back to Apple Health. Data from Apple Health is used only to show your cycle in Lyaa. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone. You can change this permission at any time in the Health app (Sharing › Apps › Lyaa).

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your name, email address or card details. To know whether you have Premium, Lyaa uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives your purchase information (which plan, dates, renewal status, your country's App Store) and a random identifier created by the app. It does not receive your name, email or any health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics tools and no selling or sharing of data, including consumer health data.
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone access.

Reminders, backups and exports

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and never leave it. Backup files and CSV exports are created only when you ask, and you choose where they go (for example Files or iCloud Drive).

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They don't include your health data.

Children

Lyaa is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EU and UK).

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete everything in Lyaa with You › Delete all my data. With iCloud sync on, this also deletes your synced data.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your iPhone.
  • To ask about or delete the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat, write to Blankmess99@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or US state laws such as California's and Washington's My Health My Data Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we don't hold your health data, most requests can be handled in the app itself. For anything else, contact us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and explain important changes in the app before they apply.
