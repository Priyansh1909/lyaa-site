Common questions

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID, open Lyaa and tap You › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Lyaa: You › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Lyaa. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Premium: turn on iCloud sync (You › Sync). Free: tap You › Create a backup file, save it to Files or iCloud Drive, then on the new phone tap You › Restore from a backup file.

Can I import my history?

If your periods are in Apple Health, tap You › Import past periods from Apple Health. Some apps don't save periods to Apple Health; then you can add your past period dates in the calendar.

How accurate are the predictions?

Tested on 1,633 real cycles, 92% of periods started inside the range Lyaa showed. Your own accuracy is in Insights. Lyaa is not a method of birth control.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that each reminder is on in You › Reminders, and that notifications for Lyaa are allowed in your iPhone's Settings › Notifications.

How do I delete my data?

You › Delete all my data removes everything from your iPhone (and your iCloud, if sync is on).
